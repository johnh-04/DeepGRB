4. ARCHITECTURAL GAP: CRUPI (RANDOM FOREST) VS. PROPOSED (XGBOOST)
================================================================================
State of the Art in DeepGRB Literature:
- Crupi's 2023 Paper (arXiv:2303.15936): Built the neural background + FOCuS
  engine; used simple heuristic cuts for classification.
- Crupi's 2024 Ph.D. Thesis (arXiv:2401.15632): Demonstrated that heuristics 
  fail on soft GRBs; explored Random Forest with XAI (SHAP) offline on 2019 data.
- Inherited Codebase State: Stopped at the heuristic rule stage (`apply_classification_logic`). 
  The machine learning classifier was never integrated end-to-end in Step 6.

Rationale for Native XGBoost Integration:
1. Handling Extreme Class Imbalance: GRBs are rare compared to background/flares. 
   XGBoost dynamically weights sparse classes via gradient optimization 
   (`scale_pos_weight`), unlike standard bagging in Random Forest.
2. Regularization & Overfitting: Built-in L1/L2 leaf regularization prevents 
   the model from overfitting on noisy Poisson count statistics.
3. Software Engineering Contribution: Transforming offline notebook exploration 
   into an integrated, automated, production-grade Step 6 classifier.
